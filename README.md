## 🕹️ About

Orc Warchief: Strategy Story is an incremental strategy game from G-DEVS.com, set in a fantasy world where a young orc warchief leads a growing Horde against human castles. Build your war machine, harvest resources from shattered defenses, upgrade warriors, and conquer fortresses across varied biomes. The game is planned for release in 2026 and this beta test gives selected players an opportunity to play early and receive a free game key.

Designed for players who enjoy active incremental strategy, resource management, fantasy sieges, army progression, and satisfying long-term upgrades.

> ⚡ **This is an exclusive beta test opportunity!** Join BETABRIDGE to get early access, help shape the game's development, and earn a free Steam key upon release.

## ✨ Features

- 🏰 **Castle Sieges** — Attack increasingly powerful human fortifications and conquer ten castles.
- ⚔️ **Horde Building** — Recruit and upgrade a growing army of warriors with distinct abilities.
- 🪨 **Resource Harvesting** — Collect stone from ruined walls and invest it in units, buildings, and upgrades.
- 🔥 **Horde Fury** — Fill the Fury bar to unleash devastating attacks and overwhelm defenders.
- 🧩 **Unit Synergies** — Combine fifteen combat unit types to unlock stronger abilities and ultimate variants.
- 💎 **Prestige Progression** — Earn gems between attempts and spend them on permanent skill-tree upgrades.
- 🎴 **Artifacts and Cards** — Choose powerful run-boosting rewards after conquering each fortress.
